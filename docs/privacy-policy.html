Section 01

Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with the Site's booking forms, self-service ROI diagnostics, calculators, or contact features, we may collect:

  • Name, professional title, and job role
  • Company name, size, and corporate website URL
  • Business email address and professional phone number
  • Monthly LLM/Cloud spend profile data (entered into the ROI calculator or intake forms)
  • Primary LLM providers and infrastructure frameworks currently in use
  • Number of active autonomous/agentic workflows running in your environment
  • Any secondary data, notes, or context you voluntarily submit via interactive forms or email correspondence

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, standard web server and analytics tools may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring URL
  • Device type

The Site uses GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub's privacy practices apply to hosting-layer data collection. See github.com/privacy for details.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through the Site is used solely for:

  • Scheduling and conducting discovery calls or pilot onboarding sessions
  • Sending confirmation and reminder communications related to booked meetings
  • Following up on expressed interest in SentryNode Gateway's services
  • Generating ROI estimates based on your inputs (processed client-side; no input data is transmitted to Provider servers unless you submit a booking or contact form)
  • Improving the Site and understanding how visitors engage with it

AI Training Restrictive Pledge: We strictly preserve the confidentiality of your professional inputs. We do not sell, rent, lease, or distribute your personal or corporate data to third parties. We do not utilize, process, or expose any information collected through this Site to train, fine-tune, or align any artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We do not "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Section 03

Pilot Program Data

If you enter a paid pilot engagement with SentryNode Gateway, data handling for your LLM inference traffic is governed separately by the executed Pilot Agreement and, where applicable, a Data Processing Agreement (DPA). The following applies to pilot participants:

  • LLM inference traffic routed through SentryNode Gateway is logged solely for governance, audit, and FinOps purposes
  • Logs are retained for 30 days post-pilot unless earlier deletion is requested
  • PII scrubbing is applied at the gateway layer on all routed requests
  • No client data is shared with third parties or used for any purpose beyond operating the gateway
  • All requests are cryptographically signed using HMAC-SHA256 and recorded in a tamper-evident Sovereign Ledger
Section 04

Data Retention

Site inquiry and booking data is retained for up to 12 months from the date of collection, or until you request deletion. Pilot program data retention is governed by the Pilot Agreement (30 days post-pilot by default). You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing legal@sentrynodegateway.com.

Right to Deletion

Section 06

Cookies & Tracking

The native code structure of this Site is statically hosted and does not independently set tracker or profiling cookies. However, third-party application modules embedded within or linked directly from the Site (such as enterprise calendar schedulers, video player embeds, GitHub links, or LinkedIn widgets) may drop persistent third-party cookies or execute background tracking scripts upon loading to facilitate their respective interactive services. These external executions are outside our operational control and are governed entirely by the privacy policies of their respective operators.

Third-party services currently embedded or integrated with this Site include:

  • Cloudflare — DNS, bot protection, and web analytics. See cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • HubSpot — Form submission and contact management. See legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy
